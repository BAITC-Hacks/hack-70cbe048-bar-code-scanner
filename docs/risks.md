# Critical risks

1. City catalog price is not branch or cash price.
2. Source freshness is not exposed.
3. Official app/API can change without notice.
4. Undocumented endpoint reuse may be disallowed.
5. Wolt explicitly restricts automated collection.
6. Retailer coverage is asymmetric; a comparison with one network is weak.
7. External barcode databases have incomplete Kazakhstan coverage.
8. Same EAN can hide pack, weight, bundle or private-label edge cases.
9. Loyalty and promotion prices can be mistaken for universal prices.
10. User photos/receipts introduce consent and personal-data obligations.

The next decision gate is not more scraping: obtain 5–10 in-store observations in Astana, then secure permission or a second approved feed. If that fails, keep the pivot as a timestamped crowd-observation/receipt product.
